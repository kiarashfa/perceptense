The

A

of

(endometrium)
when

The

When

through

over
roughly
3
to
7
days.
Average

per

30–80
ml.
Anything

above
80

What

in

flow
Menstrual

is

It

endometrial
tissue,

vaginal

and
small

of
unfertilised

The
dark

often

at
the
start
or

is
older,
oxidised
blood;
bright
red
is
fresher
flow.
Clots
up
to

size
of
a

are

Frequent

are
not,
and
they
warrant
