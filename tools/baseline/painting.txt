The

Pigment,

40,000

Painting
is
the
application
of
pigment
suspended
in
a
binder
to
a
surface
with
the
intention
of

The

are

El
Castillo,
Spain
(~40,800

and
Lascaux,
France
(~17,000

The

a
surface

is

most
fundamental
human
behaviours:
before
writing,
before
agriculture,
there
was
painting.
Understanding
painting
means
understanding
how
material
constraints,
cultural
context,
available
technology,
and
individual
genius
interact
to
produce
images
that
outlast
civilisations.
This
module
covers
every

dimension:
from
the

of

to

economics
of
Christie's,
from

to

Unlike

The

This

Walter

Every

Time

A

that
accumulates
duration
A

does

in

like

You

Yet

accumulates

—
pentimento
(painted-over

The

of

The

The

A

retains

of
its

of

the

of

Rembrandt's

—

This

from

where

The
