Fear

Fear

It

Martin

Fear

How

is
learned
and
unlearned
A
neutral
cue
becomes

when
it
reliably
predicts
pain,
humiliation,

or

Pavlov
showed

with

John

to

a

The
fear
then

to
any
white

—
a
rabbit,
a
Santa
Claus
mask.
The
cure
is
not

told
the
rat
is
safe.
The
nervous
system
needs
new
evidence,
repeated
under

until
the
cue
predicts
safety
more

than

Extinction
is

by

conditioned
fear
diminishes
when

feared

is

without

Critically,

does

original
fear

—
it

a

inhibitory
memory.
This
is
why

can
return
spontaneously
after
time
passes,
after
stress,
or
in
a

Good

therefore
varies
the
setting,

and

so

"I

this

and
still

safe."
Exposure
therapy
works
because

is

not
deletion.
The
original
fear
memory
can
return
under
stress,
in
a
new
context,
or
after
time
has
passed.
A

still

original

—
what
changes

the

is

Why

Horror

The

The

Haunted

because

The

If

About

A

Research

(Zuckerman

Terror

(Greenberg,

1973
Pulitzer-winning
"The

argues

that
human

is
largely
an
elaborate
anxiety-management
system.
Religion,
nationalism,
legacy
projects,
and
worldview
defence
all
function
to
buffer
the
terror
of
mortality.
Religions
often
organise
fear
around
death,
impurity,
punishment,
and
cosmic
judgment
—
hell
is
not

it

a

that
makes

acts

But
the
story
is
not

Buddhist
practice,
Christian
mysticism,
Stoicism,
and
Sufi

also
try
to

fear
by
changing
the
self's
relation
to
death
and
control.
Political

operates

Corey
Robin's

The

name
an

amplify
the
threat
beyond
its

and
position
the

as

only

Fear
can

into

McCarthyism,
the

War's
WMD

and

of

about

have
